Market Analysis: Document Intelligence Platforms

The document intelligence market is projected to reach $8.2B by 2028, growing at 22% CAGR.

Segment leaders offer OCR, entity extraction, and semantic search. Differentiation increasingly depends on on-device privacy and intent-aware retrieval rather than cloud-only RAG.

Customer priorities (2026 survey, n=450):
1. Data sovereignty and local-first storage (61%)
2. Natural language search over personal archives (58%)
3. Duplicate detection and corpus deduplication (34%)
4. Hybrid retrieval quality vs latency tradeoffs (29%)

IntentCloud positioning: privacy-preserving cognitive memory with Phi-3 intent parsing and Qdrant vector indexing, deployable from laptop to Raspberry Pi edge node.

Competitive moat: end-to-end pipeline (upload → extract → embed → intent search) with transparent architecture for academic and enterprise review.
